The ink rule 🖊️

The loser of every match gets marked by the winner with the temporary tattoo markers. The loser picks the spot, the winner picks the design. A quick yes or no before the pen touches skin, and nothing goes anywhere the loser didn't pick. Skin-safe markers only. If you've never used them, test a small patch first.

The non-negotiables

  • Tap = over. Double-tap your opponent or the floor, or say "tap." The match ends right away and it counts as a loss.
  • "RED" stops the whole room, not just your match.
  • 30-second check-in before every match: no-go zones, intensity 1–5, and the rule set.
  • Anyone can veto a rule set, no explanation needed. Anyone can drop out at any time.
  • Banned: strikes, anything on the neck, twisting joints, pulling hair or fingers, slams.
  • Mats down, jewelry off, nails short, water nearby. The ref doesn't wrestle and can stop anything.
